Questions

Quick answers.

macOS warns when I open Tack. Is that normal?

Yes. Tack is free and not signed with a paid Apple certificate, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

How do I allow Screen Recording?

In Tack's Settings > General press Allow… next to Screen Recording. In the list that opens, press the + button, choose Tack from Applications, turn it on and confirm with your password or Touch ID. Then quit and reopen Tack.

What do I need to run Tack?

A Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

Is Tack really native?

Yes. Tack is a native macOS app, not a website packaged in a wrapper.

Why does Tack ask for camera and microphone access?

Only for the optional webcam overlay and microphone audio. If you don't turn them on, Tack doesn't use them. See the privacy page.

Is Tack free?

Yes. Tack is free and open source. The code is on GitHub.

How do I get help?
