Questions

macOS warns when I open Tack. Is that normal?

Yes. Tack is free and isn’t signed with a paid Apple certificate, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

How do I allow Screen Recording?

In Tack’s Settings > General press Allow… next to Screen Recording. In the list that opens, press the + button, choose Tack from Applications, turn it on and confirm with your password or Touch ID. Then quit and reopen Tack.

Is Tack really free?

Yes. Tack is free and open source, and the code is on GitHub.

Does Tack collect anything?

No. There are no accounts and no analytics, and the app doesn’t use the network. Your screenshots and recordings stay on your Mac. See the privacy page.

Why does Tack ask for camera and microphone access?

Only for the optional webcam bubble and microphone audio in a recording. If you don’t turn them on, Tack doesn’t use them.

What do I need to run Tack?

A Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

How do I get help?

Open an issue on GitHub.
