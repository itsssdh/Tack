What's new in Tack.

v1.0.2Latest
October 28, 2026

Tack updates itself

  • New: Tack checks for new versions in the background and installs them for you. No more downloading and dragging the app in again.
  • New: “Check for Updates…” in the menu bar menu, and an “Install updates automatically” switch in Settings > General.
  • This is the last version you need to install by hand.

v1.0.0
October 28, 2026

First public release

  • Capture an area, a window or the whole screen, with previews you can copy, save or pin.
  • Mark up screenshots with pen, highlighter, shapes, text and blur in a built-in editor.
  • Record your screen, trim it in the video editor and export as MP4 or GIF.
  • Copy text straight from anywhere on the screen.
  • Free and open source. No account, no license, no tracking. Launches at login by default.
