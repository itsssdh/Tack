v1.0.0
October 28, 2026

First public release

  • Capture an area, a window or the whole screen, with previews you can copy, save or pin.
  • Mark up screenshots with pen, highlighter, shapes, text and blur in a built-in editor.
  • Record your screen, trim it in the video editor and export as MP4 or GIF.
  • Copy text straight from anywhere on the screen.
  • Free and open source. No account, no license, no tracking. Launches at login by default.
