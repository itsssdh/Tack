Privacy

Tack has no accounts, no analytics and doesn’t use the network. Your screenshots and recordings stay on your Mac, in the folder you choose. Here is what it asks macOS for:

  • Screen Recording: needed to take screenshots and recordings.
  • Camera: used only when you turn on the webcam bubble for a screen recording.
  • Microphone: used only when you turn on microphone audio for a screen recording.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
