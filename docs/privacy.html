Privacy

Your captures stay yours.

Tack has no accounts and no analytics. The only thing it uses the network for is checking for updates. Your screenshots and recordings stay on your Mac, in the folder you choose. Here is what it asks macOS for:

Screen RecordingNeeded to take screenshots and recordings.
CameraUsed only when you turn on the webcam bubble for a screen recording.
MicrophoneUsed only when you turn on microphone audio for a screen recording.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
