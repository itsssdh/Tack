# Privacy

Tack collects no personal information and has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

- Your screenshots and recordings stay on your Mac, in the folder you choose. They are never sent anywhere.
- The only thing the app uses the network for is checking for updates (it asks the project site for a list of versions and downloads a new one when you install it). Nothing about you or your captures is sent.
- The app asks macOS for permission to record your screen, and optionally your microphone and camera. You can withdraw these in System Settings at any time.
